Who I am

I'm studying Computer Science and Engineering and I want to end up in red teaming. Hack The Box is where most of the practice happens, so that's what most of these writeups cover.

What's here

  • Writeups of Hack The Box machines
  • Tooling I write for recon and enumeration
  • Research done on my own infrastructure

The writeups fill up fastest. The other two grow more slowly.

How I write

I write them the way I'd want to find them:

  • The enumeration output that actually mattered
  • The reasoning for each step, not just the payload
  • The attempts that failed before one worked
  • An explanation of the underlying bug when I use a public exploit

Nothing here is a copy of someone else's path through a box. Where I got stuck and read around, I say so.

Scope

Everything published here was done against machines I was authorised to test: retired Hack The Box boxes, lab environments and systems I own.
